    A few problems need a second opinion
    
      
    
        MicroTutor writes test cases for every problem, then checks them by
        breaking your solution on purpose. If a test notices the break, the
        tests are doing their job.
      
    
      
    
        For the problems below, a few of those deliberate breaks slipped past.
        That means one of two things, and MicroTutor cannot tell which on its
        own: either the break made no difference to the answer (harmless), or
        it did and a test case is missing. Until someone looks, these stay
        hidden from students.
      
    
      
      
      
      
        
        
      
      
      
      Ignoring is safe. Nothing is lost, and you can come
        back from Playground in the profile menu whenever you want.
